{/*
  The landing-page FAQ. Everything else on the landing page lives in
  `landing.ts` — see the note at the top of that file for why records belong
  in TypeScript and documents belong here. The section heading lives there
  too, as FAQ_HEADING.

  To edit: change the prose. To add a question: copy a block. The answer is
  ordinary markdown, so links, `code` and **emphasis** all work.

  <FaqItem> is injected by faq.tsx and needs no import here. It exists because
  an accordion needs to know where each answer starts and stops, and MDX gives
  a renderer no way to ask that of a heading — an earlier attempt tried to
  split on `##` boundaries and rendered nothing at all, because a compiled MDX
  component is opaque to Children.toArray. One wrapper tag per question is the
  smallest honest price for markdown answers.

  Order is page order. Keep the cheapest, most reassuring answer first — "Is
  it free?" is the question people actually arrive with.
*/}

<FaqItem q="Is it free?">
Yes. The schedule, imports, reminders, and offline mode are free. A ConPaws+
upgrade with extras is planned later — nothing you see here moves behind it.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem q="Do I need an account?">
No. ConPaws works entirely without one. Your schedule lives on your phone, not
on our servers.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem q="What if the con WiFi dies?">
Nothing happens. ConPaws is offline-first: every feature keeps working with
zero bars in a basement dealers' den.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem q="Which conventions does it work with?">
Any that publish an `.ics` calendar or a [Sched](https://sched.com) page —
which covers most furry cons. You can also build a schedule by hand for the
ones that don't.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem q="When does it launch?">
The beta opens to the waitlist first, iOS and Android together. Join above and
you'll get one email when your invite is ready.
</FaqItem>

<FaqItem q="Who makes it?">
ConPaws is built by [MrDemonWolf, Inc.](https://www.mrdemonwolf.com) —
con-goers building the app we wished we had in the hallway line.
</FaqItem>
