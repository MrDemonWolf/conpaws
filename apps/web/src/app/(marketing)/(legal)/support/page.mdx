# ConPaws Support

Need help with ConPaws or found a problem in a beta build? Email [support@conpaws.com](mailto:support@conpaws.com).

## What to Include

Please include only what is useful for reproducing the problem:

- Your device model and operating system version
- The ConPaws version and build number shown in Settings
- What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead
- A screenshot, if it helps and does not reveal anything you want to keep private

Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, or other sensitive information.

## Before You Email

- Check TestFlight for a newer ConPaws build
- Close and reopen the app
- Confirm notification permission is enabled in system settings if reminders are missing

## Privacy and Terms

Read the [Privacy Policy](/privacy) and [Terms of Service](/terms). Privacy, data, and deletion requests go to [legal@conpaws.com](mailto:legal@conpaws.com) — that is the address both documents name, and it reaches the same place.
