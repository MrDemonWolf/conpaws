# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 19, 2026

## The Short Version

ConPaws is built with privacy at its core. The current pre-release website does not accept signup submissions. If you use the app without an account, your schedules, choices, and settings stay on your device and are not sent to us. If you choose to import from a Sched link, your device contacts Sched to download that public calendar. A production build may send crash and diagnostic reports to Sentry when crash reporting is configured; the app disables Sentry's default collection of personally identifiable information. Account and ConPaws+ features are planned; if you choose them once they are available, we will collect only what's needed to provide them. We don't run ads, sell your data, or use behavioral analytics.

ConPaws ("we", "our", "us") is operated by MrDemonWolf, Inc., located at 645 3rd St, Beloit, WI, USA. Contact us at [legal@mrdemonwolf.com](mailto:legal@mrdemonwolf.com).

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the ConPaws mobile application ("the App") and the conpaws.com website.

## 2. What We Collect

### Pre-Release Waitlist (conpaws.com)

The app isn't out yet, and beta registration is not open. The current website does not accept or store signup submissions. When registration opens, we plan to record:

- **Email address** — so we can email you
- **Name** — optional; only if you fill it in
- **IP address, browser info (user agent), and country** — kept as part of the consent record, proof that you (and not a bot) signed up
- **The page address you signed up from (referrer)** — including any campaign tags (UTM parameters) it carries
- **Date and time of signup**
- **The exact consent wording shown when you submit the form**

When registration opens, this information will be stored in Cloudflare D1 (a database hosted in the US) and synced to Brevo, our email service provider (a French company), which will send the double-opt-in confirmation email and launch updates. We will not enable registration until storage, confirmation, unsubscribe, and deletion paths are working.

Once registration opens, you can leave the list through any email's unsubscribe link or by emailing [legal@mrdemonwolf.com](mailto:legal@mrdemonwolf.com) to request deletion.

### Free (No Account)

When you use ConPaws without creating an account:

- Your conventions, events, reminders, choices, and settings are stored locally on your device and are not sent to us.
- A production build may send crash and diagnostic reports to Sentry when crash reporting is configured. The app disables Sentry's default collection of personally identifiable information. We use these reports only to find and fix app problems.
- If you choose **Import from Sched**, your device requests the public calendar directly from Sched. Sched receives ordinary network request information under its [privacy policy](https://sched.com/privacy/); ConPaws does not send Sched your saved schedule choices or reminders.
- No behavioral analytics, tracking, advertising cookies, or device fingerprinting.

### Free Account

### ConPaws+ Subscribers

Once ConPaws+ is available, subscriber data is planned to include:

## 3. How We Use Your Data

We use data only for these purposes:

- **Crash diagnostics:** When enabled in a production build, identify and fix crashes and performance problems
- **Waitlist:** Once registration opens, send the confirmation email and let you know when ConPaws launches
- **Account features:** Display your profile to other users
- **ConPaws+ features:** Sync your convention data to the cloud, share your schedule publicly
- **Subscription management:** Verify your ConPaws+ subscription status

We do **not** use your data for:

- Advertising or ad targeting
- Selling to third parties
- Behavioral analytics or tracking
- Training AI or machine learning models

## 5. Data Sharing

We do **not** currently collect or share waitlist signup data. Current processing is limited to:

- **Cloudflare Workers:** Hosts this website.
- **Sched:** Downloads a public calendar only when you request a link import.
- **Sentry:** When crash reporting is configured in a production build, processes crash and diagnostic reports on our behalf. The app disables Sentry's default collection of personally identifiable information.
- **Law enforcement:** If required by law (court order, subpoena)

If the planned account, waitlist, and ConPaws+ features launch, processing would also include:

- **Apple/Google:** Authentication (OAuth sign-in) and subscription billing
- **RevenueCat:** Subscription management (receives your anonymous user ID and subscription status only)
- **Brevo:** Waitlist confirmation emails and updates (receives your email and name only)
- **Cloudflare D1/R2:** Account and waitlist records, plus avatar file storage

We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Ever.

## 6. Data Retention

| Scenario | What Happens |
|----------|-------------|
| **Free (no account)** | Your schedules, reminders, and settings stay on your device. When crash reporting is configured, diagnostics are kept only as long as needed to identify and prevent app problems, subject to the Sentry project's configured retention. |
| **Waitlist signup (once open)** | Kept until launch or until you unsubscribe — whichever comes first. |
| **Account exists** | Profile data kept until you delete your account. |
| **ConPaws+ cancelled** | Cloud data retained for 30 days, then permanently deleted. Local data stays on your device. |
| **Account deleted** | All cloud data (profile, conventions, events, avatar) permanently deleted immediately. |

## 7. Your Rights

If we begin holding your data, you have the right to:

- **Access** your data — Request a copy of the information we hold about you
- **Correct** your data — Ask us to correct inaccurate information
- **Delete** your data — Ask us to delete it; self-service account deletion is planned
- **Export** your data — Download your cloud data once account sync is available
- **Withdraw consent** — Sign out or delete your account at any time; once the waitlist opens, use any email's unsubscribe link or email us

These rights apply to all users, regardless of location. We comply with:

- **GDPR** (European Union)
- **CCPA/CPRA** (California, USA)
- **PIPEDA** (Canada)

## 8. Data Security

- Traffic to the current website is encrypted via TLS/HTTPS
- The planned Worker API will verify ownership before every D1 query; D1 does not provide Row Level Security
- Authentication will be handled by OAuth providers (Apple and Google)
- Avatar storage will use signed URLs with limited validity
- Cloudflare currently hosts the website and will host the planned D1 databases and R2 storage

## 9. Children's Privacy

ConPaws is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at [legal@mrdemonwolf.com](mailto:legal@mrdemonwolf.com) and we will delete it.
