# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** {LEGAL_LAST_UPDATED}

## The Short Version

ConPaws is built with privacy at its core. The website's waitlist is open: if you join it, we keep your email address, and what you were shown when you agreed, so we can email you about the launch. If you use the app without an account, your schedules, choices, and settings stay on your device and are not sent to us. If you choose to import from a Sched link, your device contacts Sched to download that public calendar. A production build may send crash and diagnostic reports to Sentry when crash reporting is configured; the app disables Sentry's default collection of personally identifiable information. Account and ConPaws+ features are planned; if you choose them once they are available, we will collect only what's needed to provide them. We don't run ads, sell your data, or use behavioral analytics.

## 2. What We Collect

### Pre-Release Waitlist (conpaws.com)

The app isn't out yet, but the waitlist is open. When you submit the signup form we record:

- **Email address** — so we can email you
- **Name** — optional; only if you fill it in
- **IP address, browser info (user agent), and country** — kept as part of the consent record, proof that you (and not a bot) signed up
- **The page address you signed up from (referrer)** — including any campaign tags (UTM parameters) it carries
- **Date and time of signup**
- **The exact consent wording shown when you submit the form**

This information is stored in Cloudflare D1, a database hosted in the United States. That record is the authoritative one — it is what we can produce as evidence of your consent, which is why it keeps the exact wording you were shown rather than a yes/no flag.

Your email address and name are also sent to our mailing system, which is [listmonk](https://listmonk.app/), self-hosted on our own server rather than run by a third-party marketing company. It sends the confirmation email and, later, launch updates. Delivery of those emails is handled by Amazon Simple Email Service (Amazon Web Services, US region), which processes your address in order to hand the message to your mail provider.

The list is double opt-in: nothing is sent to you beyond the confirmation email until you click the link in it. If you never confirm, your address stays unconfirmed and receives no further mail.

Before you submit the form we also run [Cloudflare Turnstile](https://www.cloudflare.com/application-services/products/turnstile/), a bot check that replaces a CAPTCHA. Cloudflare processes the challenge under its own privacy policy; it does not track you across sites.

You can leave the list at any time through the unsubscribe link in any email we send, or email [legal@conpaws.com](mailto:legal@conpaws.com) to exercise your privacy rights. When listmonk reports an unsubscribe, our next status sync marks the D1 record unsubscribed and removes its optional name, IP, browser, referrer, country, and campaign details. We keep the email and exact consent wording as suppression evidence so the address is not re-added accidentally. We do not delete listmonk subscriber records through this sync.

### Beta Testing (TestFlight and Google Play testing)

If you join a ConPaws beta:

- **If you accept an emailed TestFlight invitation,** App Store Connect shows us your name, your email address, the date you installed the build, how many sessions you have run, and how many crashes you hit. We use this only to run the beta — knowing which builds reached whom, and chasing a crash back to a tester who can reproduce it. We delete tester records when the beta programme for that build ends.
- **If you join through a public TestFlight link,** Apple does not show us your name or email address; you appear to us as an anonymous tester. Your install date, session count, and crash count are still shown.
- Google Play testing gives us the equivalent for Android testers.
- Apple and Google each process this under their own privacy policies — [Apple's](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and [Google's](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- Feedback and screenshots you send through TestFlight reach us with the device and OS details Apple attaches. Send only what you are comfortable sharing.

### Free (No Account)

When you use ConPaws without creating an account:

- Your conventions, events, reminders, choices, and settings are stored locally on your device and are not sent to us.
- A production build may send crash, performance, and other diagnostic reports to Sentry when crash reporting is configured. Reports include an installation identifier so reports from one installation can be grouped; Sentry's default collection of personally identifiable information is disabled. We use these reports only to find and fix app problems.
- When you enter a convention location, the app may send that location text to Apple's geocoding service on Apple platforms or Google's geocoding service on Android to infer its time zone. This is a platform service; ConPaws does not receive the geocoding result unless you save the convention in the app, where it stays on your device.
- If you choose **Import from Sched**, your device requests the public calendar directly from Sched. Sched receives ordinary network request information under its [privacy policy](https://sched.com/privacy/); ConPaws does not send Sched your saved schedule choices or reminders.
- No behavioral analytics, tracking, advertising cookies, or device fingerprinting.

## 5. Data Sharing

Current processing is limited to:

- **Cloudflare Workers:** Hosts this website.
- **Cloudflare D1:** Stores your waitlist record, including the consent evidence described in section 2.
- **Cloudflare Turnstile:** Checks that the signup form was submitted by a person.
- **Amazon SES:** Receives your email address in order to deliver the confirmation email and launch updates. It does not receive your consent record, IP address, or campaign tags.
- **Sched:** Downloads a public calendar only when you request a link import.
- **Sentry:** When crash reporting is configured in a production build, processes crash and diagnostic reports on our behalf. The app disables Sentry's default collection of personally identifiable information.
- **Law enforcement:** If required by law (court order, subpoena)

If the planned account and ConPaws+ features launch, processing would also include:

- **Apple/Google:** Authentication (OAuth sign-in) and subscription billing
- **RevenueCat:** Subscription management (receives your anonymous user ID and subscription status only)
- **Cloudflare D1/R2:** Account records and avatar file storage

We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Ever.
