Research → decisions

Design for the choice, not the grid.

Eight parallel tracks become eight tiny columns on a phone. This concept gives each option the full width, makes “interested” different from “going,” and helps you compare only the panels you care about. These are design hypotheses to test with attendees.

Implementation rule · Native UI first

Native controls. Supported NativeWind styles.

ConPaws already has both. Keep system controls in charge of behavior, and use NativeWind for supported styling on React Native views. SwiftUI controls use their own native props and modifiers.

01 · Discover

Time first. Full-width rows.

A short day strip keeps orientation. A visible time selector narrows the decision. Every panel shows its actual start and end, room, and an interest bookmark. All 6–10 options remain scrollable; filters never silently rank away alternatives.

Reference: Fantastical's date + agenda relationship ↗
02 · Decide

Make time for more than one.

Save several interests, then choose your own join and leave times. Attend photography until 2:25 and join the art panel at 2:35. Published event times remain visible. Overlap warnings use your attendance, and keeping an overlap remains your choice.

Reference: Sched's schedule and overlap behavior ↗
03 · Move

Your next move, at a glance.

Now shows when to leave, where to go, and when you plan to join. Larger widgets add the day’s useful stops. Watch offers quick leave-time changes. Live Activities keep the current action close. Every surface distinguishes personal times from the published schedule.

Reference: Apple lists and tables ↗
04 · Feel at home

iOS does the heavy lifting.

System type, large titles, inset grouped lists, familiar symbols, a two-option segmented control, and a dismissible sheet. Glass stays on navigation. Color has a text or icon partner. Controls use native-scale touch areas and support keyboard focus.
