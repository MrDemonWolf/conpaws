Live preview of the countdown ladder in targets/_shared/ConPawsCountdown.swift. The JavaScript below mirrors that Swift exactly, so the wording here is what a device renders. The smallest unit is a whole hour; the final hour is a single “Starting soon”.

Widgets refresh on the system's schedule, not ours. Seconds were removed because a widget cannot honour that precision, and the timeline now carries a pre-built entry for every wording change instead of a single entry plus a refresh request.
