ConPaws 1.0.0 — notes for App Review

ConPaws is a free, offline planner for fan conventions. There is no account, no login, no in-app purchase and no subscription in this version. Everything works without signing in.

How to try it

1. Open the app and tap Continue through the short introduction.
2. On "Ready for your next con?", choose one of:
   - Find my convention: lists conventions published in the ConPaws catalog. Pick one, check the dates, then tap Download schedule. (Needs an internet connection the first time; after that the schedule works offline.)
   - Import a schedule: paste a Sched schedule link or choose an .ics calendar file.
   - Explore first: skips setup. You can then tap + on the Home tab and choose Create a convention, then add events by hand.
3. Open the convention. Browse shows the schedule by day and time. Tap an event, then Add to my plan.
4. My plan shows your saved events with the free time between them. Now shows what is on now and next.
5. To test reminders, open a saved event and set a reminder. iOS asks for notification permission at that point. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on the device.
6. The Schedule tab collects saved events from every convention.

Widgets and Apple Watch

The app includes Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets and an Apple Watch app with a complication. They show your next saved event. Save at least one event that has not started yet, then add the ConPaws widget from the Home Screen gallery.

Data and privacy

Convention schedules, saved events, reminders and settings are stored only on the device. The app does not track users and shows no ads. Crash and performance diagnostics are sent to Sentry, are not linked to identity, and contain no personal information. The app makes no other server requests except to download a convention schedule from api.conpaws.com when the user asks for one, or from the Sched link the user enters.

The app uses only standard encryption (HTTPS), declared as exempt.
